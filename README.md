# 🌾 AgriTrade AI – Agentic Agricultural Micro-Economies & Supply Arbitrage Platform

**AgriTrade AI** is an intelligent, multi-agent agricultural technology platform designed for smallholder farmers and farming cooperatives. The platform continuously monitors hyper-local crop yields, weather risks, market price spreads, logistics costs, and buyer demand to recommend optimal economic actions—such as immediate harvesting, multi-market arbitrage, freight pooling, or deferred cold storage.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & AI Agents

The platform is driven by 9 specialized, inter-connected AI agents:

1. **🌾 Crop Intelligence Agent:** Analyzes farm acreage, historical yields, crop quality grades, and regional micro-supply surpluses.
2. **🌦️ Weather Risk Agent:** Tracks real-time micro-climate indicators (rainfall probability, humidity, temperature) to trigger urgent harvesting advisories.
3. **📈 Market Intelligence Agent:** Evaluates gross market prices across local, district, and regional mandis, deducting transport and spoilage losses to compute true net return ($\text{Net Return} = \text{Price} - \text{Transport} - \text{Storage} - \text{Loss}$).
4. **💡 Supply Arbitrage Engine:** Identifies real-time price variances between markets and calculates potential added revenue ($\text{₹/kg}$).
5. **🚚 Logistics & Freight Pooling Agent:** Consolidates micro-shipments from neighboring farms within the same village cluster to reduce per-unit transportation costs.
6. **🏪 Smart Storage & Inventory Agent:** Models expected future price surges against cold storage fees to determine if holding inventory yields higher net profits.
7. **🤝 Buyer Discovery Agent:** Algorithms match available crop supply with commercial buyer requirements based on volume, quality grade, and location.
8. **💬 AI Negotiation Agent:** Simulates interactive price negotiations between farmers and prospective buyers using breakeven margins and market benchmark ceilings.
9. **🧠 Central Decision Engine:** Synthesizes multi-agent feeds into clear, actionable advisories with full step-by-step explainability.
